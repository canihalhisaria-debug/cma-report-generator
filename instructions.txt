Run this to generate a new fixed workbook (without overwriting original):

python3 cma_fix.py --cc-amount 10000000 --roi 11

Only these two inputs are required:
1) CC Amount
2) ROI

Auto-calculated:
- Sales
- Interest
- Stock
- Debtors
- Creditors
- Projected P&L
- Projected Balance Sheet
- Working Capital Analysis
- Financial Ratios

Stock logic applied:
- Year 1 closing stock = Year 2 opening stock
